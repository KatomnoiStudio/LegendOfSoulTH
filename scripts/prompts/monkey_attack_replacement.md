# Monkey King attack replacement frames

Built-in ImageGen was used with three image roles:

1. `monkey-anim-0.png` — absolute identity, anatomy, costume, and body-scale reference.
2. Newly approved frame 12 — visual identity reference for the new attack sequence.
3. The corresponding old attack frame — pose reference only.

Prompt template:

> Create the exact same Monkey King character as the identity references in the requested attack pose. Redraw a new clean production game-sprite frame. Keep identical facial identity, head size, shoulder width, torso thickness, limb thickness, hand and foot size, staff thickness, costume construction, palette, and rendering. Use a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background. Keep one full-body character, a consistent pelvis/torso anchor, and a common ground baseline. Do not zoom or change camera scale. Do not add motion trails, dust, shadows, text, watermarks, duplicate limbs, or baked effects.

Pose set:

- Frame 12: crouching ready pose with the staff angled behind the upper body.
- Frame 13: very low grounded crouch, one hand touching the ground and one leg extended.
- Frame 14: wide fighting stance with the staff raised diagonally behind the shoulder.
- Frame 15: wide squat with both arms holding the staff overhead.
- Frame 16: forward horizontal staff spin/thrust at chest height.
- Frame 17: side-profile follow-through of the horizontal staff twirl.
